DE-RENDER: QUANTUM SOUL & BIOMETRIC IDENTITY ERASURE
====================================================

REQUIREMENTS
------------
1. ScriptHookV
2. ScriptHookVDotNet v3 (Nightly / v3.6+)

INSTALLATION
------------
1. Make sure you have a "scripts" folder in your main Grand Theft Auto V directory.
2. Copy the following files from this folder into your "Grand Theft Auto V/scripts/" folder:
   - DeRendered.dll
   - DeRendered.baked.txt
   - DeRendered.ini

HOW TO USE
----------
- Press [F10] in-game to toggle De-Render ON or OFF.
- Approach pedestrians (within 9 meters) with clear line-of-sight to collapse their quantum state and view their live biometric HUD.

CONFIGURATION
-------------
You can edit "DeRendered.ini" in your "scripts" folder to customize:
- ToggleKey (Default: F10)
- ScanRadius (Default: 70.0)
- MaxTargets (Default: 28)
- PhosphorGreen RGB accent colors
